Technology stocks are leading the charge with semiconductor and storage plays accelerating on AI infrastructure demand—SNDK (+8.3%), STX (+7.9%), and INTC (+5.4%) all benefited from strong earnings and robust data center tailwinds. Software names like DDOG (+6.3%), ORCL (+6.5%), and SHOP (+5.4%) extended gains on positive sector forecasts and AI-driven product innovations, while financial services outperformed with CBOE (+9.0%) and AIG (+5.3%) posting strong earnings. The sole significant casualty was CLX (-9.7%), which missed guidance expectations and exposed weakness in consumer defensive stocks ahead of potential margin pressure.

Recommendation

Buy:** Continue accumulating AI-exposed semiconductor and enterprise software names (INTC, ORCL, DDOG) on any weakness—the earnings momentum and infrastructure demand are genuine. **Avoid:** Consumer defensive and healthcare names showing operational headwinds (CLX, SYK) until guidance stabilizes. **Hold:** Recent momentum in MSTR and PSKY reflects speculative strength; take profits on +7% days and wait for pullbacks before re-entering.
