Market SummaryRead ▾

U.S.-Iran escalation fears triggered a broad tech selloff overnight, with semiconductor and storage stocks leading losses: MU (-9.9%), WDC (-8.6%), COHR (-9.8%), and LRCX (-5.4%) all hit by supply chain disruption concerns and DRAM weakness. Beyond chips, SYY crashed 15.3% on its massive $29B Jetro acquisition debt concerns, while BSX tumbled 9.0% following a Raymond James downgrade. ServiceNow (+5.6%) bucked the trend on AI partnerships, and Insmed (+5.5%) soared after Morgan Stanley hiked its price target 28%, but these were exceptions in a risk-off environment as geopolitical premiums pushed oil toward $100 and broad indices into correction territory.

Recommendation

Avoid semiconductor equipment and memory stocks (MU, LRCX, TER, COHR) until geopolitical clarity emerges—downside risks from supply disruption and DRAM spot weakness outweigh near-term AI upside. SYY is a sell on integration execution risk in a rising-rate environment; LEN and cyclicals face continued pressure if energy volatility persists. Selectively nibble NOW and high-quality software names on dips, as today's panic may overdiscount long-term AI fundamentals, but wait for a geopolitical resolution before rotating back into hardware-dependent sectors.
