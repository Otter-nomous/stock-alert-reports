Market SummaryRead ▾

Tech and Healthcare are dominating overnight action, with semiconductor and AI plays particularly strong: ARM (+10.8%), SOXL (+3.05%), SMCI (+8.1%), and ORCL (+6.7%) all benefiting from AI infrastructure demand tailwinds. Earnings beats across sectors—AXON (+12.3%), A (+16.9%), BBY (+15.8%), HRL (+12.5%), IQV (+9.3%)—show investors rewarding solid execution, while SNPS's 8.6% decline despite beating earnings signals valuation fatigue in pricey names. SaaS sector momentum is solid following Snowflake's beat, lifting SHOP (+7.9%), NOW (+6.5%), but weakness in TTD (-5.1%) and TSN (-6.1%) suggests selective tape with isolated pockets of concern.

Recommendation

Buy into semiconductor and AI infrastructure strength (ARM, SMCI, ORCL) on this dip—today's moves confirm sustained institutional conviction in the mega-trend. Rotate away from richly-valued software names like SNPS; take profits on >10% gainers like AXON, A, and BBY that have already priced in beats. Avoid defensive plays until tech consolidates, but monitor Healthcare (TMO, IQV, WAT) as a tactical hold given solid earnings and M&A catalysts.
