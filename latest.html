Market SummaryRead ▾

Memory and optical semiconductor stocks are on fire, with LITE (+16.5%) surging on Nasdaq-100 inclusion, COHR (+13.3%), GLW (+10.9%), QCOM (+8.4%), and MU (+6.5%) all riding AI infrastructure tailwinds. The chip sector's strength is spreading into complementary plays—VRT (+8.2%) on data center cooling demand, and DOW (+5.1%) on liquid cooling fluids. However, weakness is concentrated in software (WDAY -5.0% amid activist pressure, SHOP -7.1% on weak guidance) and consumer (DG -7.6%, TGT -5.4%), while healthcare faces headwinds (ISRG -6.7%, IDXX -5.0% after BofA downgrade). Profit-taking is also hitting DELL (-5.2%) despite its 107% YTD run.

Recommendation

Stay long semiconductor and optical infrastructure names—the AI capex cycle remains intact with tight memory supply and record-high storage demand. Avoid SHOP, WDAY, and consumer discretionary names showing both fundamental and sentiment deterioration; the consumer is cracking. DELL should be avoided ahead of earnings despite strong fundamentals, as the 107% rally has priced in optimism; wait for a pullback or confirmed earnings beat before re-entering.
