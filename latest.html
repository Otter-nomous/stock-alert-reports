Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad-based semiconductor strength is driving today's market, with memory chip stocks (MU +10.4%, SNDK +10.9%) and equipment makers (LRCX +6.5%, AMAT +5.1%) surging on AI infrastructure demand and tight supply dynamics. Intel's +12.4% gain reflects investor rotation into chipmakers beyond Nvidia, while Qualcomm (+9.9%) benefits from strategic confidence following its veteran executive's appointment to lead Intel's AI efforts. However, weakness in e-commerce (SHOP -15.0%) and select tech names (PLTR -7.0%) signals investors are rotating away from unprofitable growth stories, with PayPal (-8.3%) also punished despite solid fundamentals due to turnaround skepticism. Healthcare and industrials show pockets of strength with beat-and-raise results (WAT +13.0%, ROK +8.9%, IQV +8.8%), while defensive sectors lag.

Recommendation

Buy semiconductor equipment and memory chip plays on this momentum—the supply/demand dislocation for AI infrastructure remains genuine, and valuations have room to run. Avoid or trim positions in unprofitable growth stories lacking near-term path to profitability (SHOP, PLTR); rotation from these names appears structural, not tactical. Hold quality industrial and healthcare names (ROK, IQV, CRL) showing earnings beats, but avoid those with execution concerns despite headline beats (APTV, LDOS showing post-spike weakness).
