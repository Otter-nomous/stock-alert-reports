Market SummaryRead ▾

Major indices are underwater across the board—SPY -1.71%, QQQ -1.95%, and leveraged plays TQQQ and SOXL down 5.94% and 4.82% respectively—as a combination of geopolitical tension (U.S.-Iran escalation), AI sector disruption concerns (Anthropic's Claude leak triggering software/cybersecurity worries), and sector-specific headwinds trigger a risk-off move. Technology is the hardest hit with DDOG -7.9%, ARM -6.9%, and the entire cybersecurity complex (PANW -6.0%, CRWD -5.9%) down on commoditization fears, while travel stocks (ABNB -6.2%, NCLH -6.9%, LUV -5.5%) suffer from soaring fuel costs tied to Middle East tensions. The sole bright spot: ETR +6.8% on a $2.65B Meta power deal, signaling selective strength in AI infrastructure plays.

Recommendation

Avoid catching falling knives in software and cybersecurity until the Claude AI panic settles—wait for capitulation signals before re-entering DDOG, PANW, or CRWD. Travel and airline names (ABNB, LUV, NCLH) face structural headwinds that won't reverse quickly; skip near-term. **Buy the dip selectively** in AI infrastructure (utilities, data centers) and established semiconductor plays with direct Meta/NVIDIA exposure, but stay defensive overall until geopolitical risk clarifies.
