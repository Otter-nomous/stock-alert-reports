Market SummaryRead ▾

Technology stocks dominated overnight trading following Nvidia CEO's reassurance that AI won't disrupt software incumbents, triggering a broad sector rally. Dell Technologies led with a stunning +32.8% jump after Nvidia announced RTX Spark processors will ship in Dell laptops by fall 2026, while NetApp (+22.4%) beat earnings and raised guidance on enterprise AI storage demand. Software names rallied hard—Workday +12.4%, ServiceNow +14.4%, Oracle +10.8%—alongside infrastructure plays like HPE (+12.6%) announcing AI-optimized servers. The only notable tech loser was Intel (-5.1%), directly threatened by Nvidia's new PC processor architecture.

Recommendation

Take profits on yesterday's biggest winners (Dell, NetApp, IBM) given the 20%+ intraday moves—these look overbought into a pre-market session. Rotate into quality software names like Datadog (+9.8%, FedRAMP catalyst) and CrowdStrike (+8.9%, strong earnings ahead) that have real growth drivers beyond sentiment. Avoid Intel until it articulates a credible competitive response; the competitive threat is structural, not cyclical.
