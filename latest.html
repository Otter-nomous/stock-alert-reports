Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks dominated overnight trading, with ARM exploding 16.4% on its launch of proprietary data center chips challenging Intel and AMD in AI infrastructure—a critical inflection point in the competitive landscape. Intel and AMD both gained 7.1% and 7.3% respectively on pricing power announcements, while Super Micro Computer surged 8.2% aided by geopolitical relief and short covering. Beyond semiconductors, healthcare names ALNY (+6.7%) and INSM (+6.6%) rallied on clinical wins and revenue guidance raises, while HPE (+7.9%) benefited from enterprise AI infrastructure momentum. The broad-based strength pushed QQQ +0.66%, TQQQ +1.77%, and the semiconductor-heavy SOXL +3.78%, signaling sustained appetite for growth and AI-adjacent equities.

Recommendation

ARM's 16.4% move warrants caution despite the compelling narrative—the stock has likely priced in initial enthusiasm and near-term pullback risk is elevated. AMD and Intel appear more reasonably valued following single-digit gains backed by concrete margin improvement catalysts; both merit holds for investors already positioned. The healthcare moves (ALNY, INSM) are driven by specific clinical/commercial catalysts rather than sector rotation, making them selective opportunities for due diligence rather than broad sector plays; avoid chasing late after overnight moves.
