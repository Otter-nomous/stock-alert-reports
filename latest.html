Market SummaryRead ▾

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's Computex keynote ignited a semiconductor and infrastructure supercycle narrative, lifting optical/photonics plays (COHR +17.6%, GLW +13.4%, LITE +13.7%) and chipmakers (MRVL +32.5%, AMAT +7.0%, LRCX +5.5%, KLAC +5.4%) while semiconductor equipment broadly rallied. HPE surged 19.5% on AI networking credentials, and power/cooling suppliers (GNRC +5.7%, FIX +5.3%) benefited from data center buildout expectations. However, profit-taking hit software names (WDAY -5.3%, NOW -6.0%, ACN -5.3%) and high-flyers (SHOP -5.7%), while regulatory headwinds pressured exchanges (CBOE -8.4%, NDAQ -5.3%) and AI substitution fears hit INTU (-8.9%) and EPAM (-5.1%).

Recommendation

Buy semiconductor equipment (AMAT, LRCX, KLAC) and optical infrastructure (GLW, COHR) as the AI capex cycle appears structural, not cyclical. Avoid software and services names showing profit-taking weakness—FDS, EPAM, and ACN face legitimate displacement risks from AI. Hold exchange operators (CBOE, NDAQ) until regulatory clarity emerges; the futures approval poses real structural headwinds.
