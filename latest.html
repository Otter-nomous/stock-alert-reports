Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor and chip-adjacent stocks dominated overnight gains as investors rotated back into AI plays following last week's selloff. Intel (+11.2%) soared on reports of a major Google AI chip order, validating its foundry ambitions and TSMC diversification narrative, while Micron (+9.9%), Marvell (+9.6%), and equipment makers KLA (+9.3%) and Lam Research (+7.0%) all surged on sector-wide recovery and positive guidance. Beyond chips, Corning (+5.6%) benefited from Amazon's multibillion-dollar data center deal, and Coinbase (+6.4%) rode Bitcoin's rebound above $63K, signaling renewed institutional appetite for risk assets. The broad-based tech rally lifted QQQ +1.56% and TQQQ +4.41%, with semiconductor equipment stocks (ASML +6.5%, COHR +6.6%) particularly strong as cloud infrastructure buildout accelerates.

Recommendation

Chip and semiconductor equipment names look extended after such violent overnight moves—take profits on outsized gainers like INTC and MRVL rather than chase. Instead, watch for sector consolidation and look for entry points in quality mega-cap tech (SPY/QQQ holdings) that haven't yet participated fully. Avoid chasing MSTR and crypto plays on sentiment alone; stick with semiconductor exposure only if you have conviction on multi-quarter AI capex cycles, and favor ASML and LRCX over single-catalyst plays.
