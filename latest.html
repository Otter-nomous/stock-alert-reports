Market SummaryRead ▾

Markets surge across the board following Trump's U.S.-Iran military action suspension, with SPY +2.55%, QQQ +2.97%, and SOXL +19.36% overnight. The semiconductor complex is the clear winner—INTC +11.4%, MPWR +10.2%, LRCX +9.9%, AMAT +8.9%—driven by geopolitical de-escalation unlocking helium supplies and revived supply chain confidence, plus BofA's $1.3 trillion 2026 chip demand outlook. Optical and AI infrastructure plays accelerated alongside: COHR +10.5%, CIEN +10.3%, GLW +11.2%, and META +6.5% on its $21B CoreWeave data center commitment. Energy names collapsed in inverse (APA -9.8%, MPC -5.5%) as crude plummeted 17%, while industrials and travel stocks rallied on lower fuel costs and reduced war premium (CCL +11.2%, UAL +7.9%, LUV +6.7%).

Recommendation

BUY the semiconductor consolidation play:** LRCX, AMAT, and KLAC offer safer exposure to the AI chip capex cycle with the geopolitical uncertainty removed and analyst upgrades fresh. **AVOID** high-valuation software names like PLTR (-6.2% on Burry's bubble warning) and WDAY/-INTU (-6.5%/-5.1% on Anthropic's Managed Agents threat)—rotation out of stretched multiples is real. **WATCH** energy stocks for capitulation oversold levels; APA and MPC may find support as markets digest lower oil, but avoid catching this knife near-term.
