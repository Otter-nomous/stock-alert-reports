Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks are commanding today's rally, with memory plays MU (+8.9%), INTC (+8.8%), and SNDK (+9.0%) leading alongside optical networking names LITE (+8.8%) and CIEN (+7.0%) on renewed AI infrastructure demand. MRVL (+7.7%) benefited specifically from NVIDIA's $2B equity injection and expanded partnership. The broader tech strength pushed QQQ +1.24% and TQQQ +3.72%, while the semiconductor-heavy SOXL surged +9.08%. Offsetting these gains, NKE plummeted -15.5% on weak China demand and elevated oil costs, and APTV (-10.6%) joined the consumer cyclical selloff, signaling rotational weakness in that sector.

Recommendation

Ride the semiconductor momentum: MU, INTC, and LITE appear well-positioned on structural AI/cloud tailwinds and offer entry points after this move. Avoid NKE until clarity emerges on China recovery and margin relief; the -15.5% drop may signal further downside if guidance remains troubled. Energy and real estate remain pressured (TPL -7.5%, ARE -6.7%) — stay selective in these sectors unless valuations prove compelling.
