Market SummaryRead ▾

Technology stocks dominated overnight gains, with semiconductor and infrastructure plays leading the charge: DELL (+16.8%), HPQ (+15.3%), NTAP (+12.4%), SWKS (+12.1%), and QCOM (+11.6%) surged on AI server demand, earnings beats, and easing trade tensions. The semiconductor-heavy SOXL jumped 6.82% while QQQ and TQQQ climbed 0.42% and 1.16% respectively. Beyond chips, ROST (+8.1%) crushed earnings with 17% EPS upside and 20.6% revenue growth, F (+9.2%) rallied on EV strategy optimism, and EL (+11.9%) bounced after killing the Puig merger. Falling Treasury yields sparked a risk-on rotation into growth equities, lifting SaaS names like WDAY and TTD.

Recommendation

Buy the dip in quality semis and AI infrastructure plays** — QCOM, NXPI, and HPE show genuine momentum from earnings and guidance, not just sentiment. **Avoid chasing pre-earnings euphoria in DELL and HPQ** — their 16-15% overnight pops ahead of earnings Thursday carry elevated reversal risk if results disappoint. **Hold ROST and defensive cyclicals** as genuine earnings beats justify valuations, but monitor macro headwinds given the yield-driven rally may be fragile.
