Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks dominated overnight trading on the back of explosive AI demand catalysts. Micron (MU) surged 21.3% after UBS tripled its price target, citing AI memory chip positioning, while the broader chip complex rallied in sympathy—SOXL jumped 18.5%, with equipment plays KLAC (+6.5%), LRCX (+5.6%), and AMAT (+5.1%) all beating guidance and signaling extended demand through 2027. Analyst upgrades flooded the tape across memory (SNDK +9.2%, WDC +7.6%), processors (AMD +7.7%, ON +9.4%), and equipment (TER +8.1%), creating a clear bull case for AI infrastructure buildout. Meanwhile, consumer cyclicals stumbled—AutoZone (AZO) dropped 10.7% despite earnings, and Tractor Supply (TSCO) fell 5.7% on weak pet spending trends.

Recommendation

Buy** the semiconductor equipment and memory value play at these levels—KLAC's 10-for-1 split, 21% dividend raise, and multi-year demand signal make it a core holding, while WDC and MU offer leveraged exposure to the AI data center acceleration with reasonable entry points after the gap-up. **Avoid** consumer cyclicals like AZO and TSCO where earnings beats are being punished; the macro headwinds in discretionary spending are real and contrarian to today's risk-on sentiment.
