Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor and infrastructure plays dominated overnight gains, with MRVL (+7.1%) riding BofA's AI compute upgrade, SMCI (+8.8%) surging on new server launches, and COHR (+8.2%) rallying on silicon carbide advances for AI datacenters. However, software and services took heavy hits—NOW fell 7.6% on UBS downgrade, PANW dropped 6.7% amid regulatory distractions, and AKAM cratered 16.7% on Anthropic's Claude Agents launch sparking edge computing displacement fears. FICO's 14% plunge reflects regulatory pressure on pricing models as AI alternatives emerge, signaling investor rotation from software licensing to hardware enablement.

Recommendation

Buyers should target semiconductor infrastructure names like MRVL and SMCI showing genuine execution momentum and analyst upgrades, while avoiding enterprise software names like NOW and PANW where valuations haven't adjusted to competitive or regulatory headwinds. The 16.7% AKAM sell-off may be overdone short-term, but the underlying thesis—that AI agents displace edge services—warrants caution on legacy cloud infrastructure plays until they prove adaptability.
