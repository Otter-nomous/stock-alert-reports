Market SummaryRead ▾

The market is decisively betting on AI infrastructure dominance, with semiconductor and data center plays crushing it across the board. SMCI (+24.5%), AMD (+18.6%), and ARM (+13.6%) led a tech avalanche fueled by earnings beats and supply constraint commentary signaling insatiable AI demand—GLW (+12.0%), LRCX (+7.7%), and TER (+7.1%) followed suit on equipment tailwinds. Meanwhile, energy stocks cratered on Trump's Iran nuclear deal prospects reducing crude supply urgency: FANG, DVN, APA, OXY all down 5-8%. Healthcare saw a split decision (DVA +23.5% beats, COR -17.4% misses), while industrials and discretionary benefited from both AI exposure (DELL +10.4%, CARR +5.8%) and macro relief (LEN +5.0% on Fed rate cut hopes).

Recommendation

Buy the AI infrastructure dip-proof names:** SMCI, TER, LRCX, and GLW have earned their rallies on actual execution and forward visibility—hold through any pullback as capex cycles have years left. **Avoid energy entirely** until geopolitical clarity emerges; the risk/reward is inverted with Iran deal hopes crushing near-term pricing power. **Sell CDW (-20.3%) weakness as a sector warning sign** if broader PC/server sales slow despite data center strength; watch for similar red flags in software/services. **Stay selective in healthcare:** DVA's +23.5% beat is real, but COR's -17.4% miss signals margin compression concerns—vet fundamentals stock-by-stock before adding.
