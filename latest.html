Market SummaryRead ▾

Mega-cap tech declined modestly (SPY -0.31%, QQQ -0.12%) but semiconductor weakness dragged heavily, with SOXL plunging 8.29% after ARM's demand warning and Marvell's Celestial AI order cancellation. Cybersecurity and AI infrastructure stocks decisively outperformed: DDOG exploded 31.3% on $1B+ revenue and AI wins, FTNT surged 20% on elevated demand, and PANW rose 7% on strengthened channel checks. Healthcare suffered dual blows—INSM cratered 23.4% on BRINSUPRI launch disappointment and ZTS tanked 21.5% after cutting guidance—while industrial/aerospace names (AXON +10.6%, HWM +6.3%) and enterprise software (WDAY +6.7%, NOW +5.1%) rode earnings strength.

Recommendation

Buy cybersecurity dip-buyers: PANW, CRWD, and FTNT demonstrate sustained demand momentum despite valuations, making them defensive tech plays. Avoid chip exposure near-term—ARM's demand warning and SOXL's weakness signal sector fatigue after extended rallies. Selectively trim healthcare longs (ZTS, INSM) on guidance misses, but accumulate quality industrials like AXON showing resilience with raised guidance despite tariff headwinds.
