Market SummaryRead ▾

The broad tech selloff intensified overnight, with QQQ dropping 2.39% and semiconductor-heavy TQQQ plummeting 7.12%, driven primarily by Google's announcement of TurboQuant—an AI algorithm that dramatically reduces memory requirements for large language models. This sparked a cascade of selling across memory and semiconductor equipment makers: LRCX (-9.4%), MU (-7.0%), WDC (-7.7%), STX (-8.3%), and KLAC (-6.0%) all cratered on disruption fears. Beyond semiconductors, META (-8.0%) fell further amid social media addiction lawsuit liability, while industrial and equipment stocks (VRT -8.6%, CARR -7.7%, LII -9.0%) deteriorated on geopolitical Iran tensions pushing oil prices higher and broader large-cap growth anxiety. Only energy stocks outperformed—VLO gained 5.8%—as rising bond yields and oil prices provided tailwinds.

Recommendation

Avoid semiconductor equipment and memory names through this near-term volatility; the TurboQuant narrative will likely dominate technicals until management commentary surfaces. Consider rotating into defensive energy plays (VLO) and waiting for capitulation in high-quality semiconductor equipment names (LRCX, KLAC) before re-entering—current panic pricing may offer entry points within 1-2 weeks if no additional disruption catalysts emerge. Stay away from META until litigation clarity improves.
