Market SummaryRead ▾

Micron's blowout earnings and UBS price target hike to $1,625 triggered a semiconductor-wide surge, with MU gaining 19.3% and dragging the entire chip ecosystem higher. Semiconductor equipment makers (AMAT +5.3%, LRCX +5.7%, KLAC +6.5%) and memory peers (SNDK +7.5%, ON +9.3%) rallied on undersupply signals, while AMD (+7.8%) and TXN (+5.1%) benefited from AI data center tailwinds and strong earnings guidance. The tech-heavy QQQ jumped 1.78% overnight and SOXL surged 18.49%, while broader markets (SPY +0.66%, SPX +0.61%) trailed the semiconductor outperformance—signaling concentrated momentum in AI infrastructure plays.

Recommendation

Ride momentum in tier-one semiconductor equipment makers (AMAT, LRCX, KLAC) and foundational AI chip suppliers (AMD, TXN) rather than chasing MU at inflated valuations after a 19% gap-up. Avoid consumer-cyclical weakness (AZO -9.0% on margin pressure, TSCO -5.8% on weak pet spending downgrade)—sentiment divergence suggests rotation into AI winners is real. Consider UAL (+6.0%) as a tactical long on oil crash tailwinds, but size positions accordingly given today's outsized tech concentration risk.
