Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks led a broad tech rally overnight, with Intel (+23.6%), AMD (+13.9%), and QCOM (+11.1%) driving gains on renewed AI optimism and strategic partnerships—notably QCOM's reported collaboration with OpenAI on AI smartphone processors. The chip strength cascaded through design tools (SNPS +9.6%, CDNS +5.9%) and equipment makers (KLAC +6.6%), lifting the QQQ +1.91% and TQQQ +5.64%. Meanwhile, cable and telecom took heavy losses: CHTR plunged 25.5% on earnings misses and subscriber declines, while CMCSA dropped 12.9% following analyst downgrades citing fiber/satellite competition. Broader strength in energy (BKR beat Q1), utilities (CEG +7.1% on Calpine acquisition), and select healthcare (EW +5.6%) rounded out the session.

Recommendation

Buy semiconductor strength selectively**—QCOM and design/equipment names (SNPS, KLAC) appear undervalued after today's moves given structural AI tailwinds; SNDK's 44% PT raise suggests memory demand has legs. **Avoid or exit cable/telecom exposure entirely**: CHTR and CMCSA face secular headwinds that today's selloffs only begin to reflect. **Hold tech broadly** given QQQ's momentum, but trim positions in value traps like BSX (guidance cut) and overextended names showing late-day fatigue.
