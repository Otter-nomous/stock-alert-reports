Semiconductor bloodbath: SK Hynix-Nvidia deal triggers 30%+ sector collapse.
Market SummaryRead ▾

The semiconductor sector is in freefall, with SOXL down 30.51% overnight following SK Hynix's exclusive AI memory partnership with Nvidia—a direct threat to Micron's (MU) high-bandwidth memory dominance. Memory and chip design stocks cratered across the board: MU -13.3%, Teradyne (TER) -12.0%, Intel (INTC) -11.3%, AMD -10.9%, and equipment makers LRCX -9.8% and AMAT -9.7%. Broadcom's disappointing earnings guidance and stronger-than-expected jobs data (stoking Fed rate-hike fears) accelerated the selloff, dragging down the broader Nasdaq (QQQ -4.80%) and foundational AI infrastructure plays like NVDA (-6.2%) and Oracle (-9.6%). This is a valuation reset, not a demand problem—AI capex remains robust, but the market is repricing risk.

Recommendation

Avoid** the entire semiconductor complex today; let capitulation complete before re-entry. **Buy the dip selectively** in 48–72 hours on mega-cap AI beneficiaries (NVDA, AMD, ASML) and memory plays (MU) once short-covering stabilizes. **Hold cash** and watch for support at SPY -3.5% to -4.0%; if breached, broader economic concerns emerge. **Defensive rotation plays** (FDXF +6.4%, CLX +5.0%) are pricing in prolonged uncertainty—avoid chasing them into resistance.
