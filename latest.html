Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad-based overnight gains across major indices (SPY +1.05%, QQQ +1.02%, leveraged plays TQQQ +3.46% and SOXL +3.70%) were driven by two primary catalysts: Trump's de-escalation signals with Iran and continued investor enthusiasm for AI-related businesses. Geopolitical relief sparked a rally in cyclical and energy stocks—cruise lines (NCLH +6.2%, RCL +5.8%, CCL +5.5%), materials (ALB +6.9%, FCX +5.5%), and energy (SLB +5.6%)—while tech benefited from both sentiment and sector-specific wins: PLTR +6.7% on Pentagon's Maven AI adoption, Q +6.8% on AI semiconductor capacity expansion, and SMCI +5.1% on valuation reassessment. The notable exception was EL (−7.7%), which fell on Puig merger concerns, and FICO (−5.7%), pressured by deteriorating credit metrics in housing.

Recommendation

Buy:** AI infrastructure plays (PLTR, Q, SMCI, CIEN) remain attractive on genuine fundamental catalysts beyond sentiment; PLTR's Pentagon adoption is especially defensible. **Sell/Avoid:** EL should be sidelined until merger terms clarify, and FICO weakness signals potential credit stress—monitor housing data closely before adding cyclicals. **Hold:** Geopolitical-driven gains in cruise and energy are sentiment-dependent; take partial profits on outsized movers (NCLH, RCL, SLB) as Iran headlines fade.
