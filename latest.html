Market SummaryRead ▾

The semiconductor and optical networking sectors are leading overnight gains, with SOXL surging 6.79% on broad chip strength. MRVL (+8.2%), ON (+11.1%), COHR (+7.9%), and ARM (+6.4%) are driving the momentum on analyst upgrades, AI data center demand acceleration, and server processor share gains. However, significant cracks are forming elsewhere: software and applications are struggling with APP down 7.6% despite earnings, ACN and IT both down 6.0% despite positive results, and healthcare suffering across PODD, MRNA, and TECH. The divergence suggests investors are rotating into hardware-centric AI plays while taking profits in higher-valuation software and biotech names.

Recommendation

BUY:** Semiconductor and optical infrastructure names (ON, MRVL, COHR) remain attractive as AI capex cycle strength and margin expansion catalysts support continued outperformance. **SELL/AVOID:** Software and application stocks (APP, ACN, IT) showing earnings disappointment despite solid results signal valuation compression; healthcare names (PODD, MRNA, TECH) face both sector headwinds and company-specific issues. Consider rotating from beaten-down software into hardware beneficiaries where analyst upgrades and structural demand support valuations.
