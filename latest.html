Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad market strength overnight with SPY +0.99% and QQQ +0.93%, but semiconductor and AI-adjacent plays decisively outperformed—SOXL +7.64%, TQQQ +2.72%. QCOM (+15.1%) and PWR (+15.8%) led on earnings beats and raised guidance tied to hyperscaler capex; GOOG/GOOGL (+10%) validated AI spending with strong Cloud growth; Alphabet's gains offset META's -8.6% plunge over announced infrastructure layoffs. Industrials rallied hard on data center tailwinds: CAT +9.9%, CARR +8.8% (500% surge in DC orders), EME +7%, FIX +6.7%. Semiconductors followed QCOM's lead (AMD +5.2%, MPWR +5.7%, WDC +5.3%), while optical comms surged (CIEN +11%, GLW +8.1%, LITE +5.1%) on accelerating AI data center demand.

Recommendation

Buy:** Semiconductor and infrastructure plays (MPWR, TER +12.1%, SWKS +12%) remain attractive given durable AI capex cycles and analyst upgrades; industrials positioned for sustained DC demand (CARR, EME, FIX, PWR) offer multi-quarter tailwinds. **Hold/Avoid:** META's -8.6% signals market skepticism of burn-heavy AI strategies without near-term ROI clarity—wait for margin guidance before re-entry. **Monitor:** Broad tech upside hinges on sustained earnings beats; rotation into Magnificent 7 +10% moves risks mean reversion if forward guidance softens.
