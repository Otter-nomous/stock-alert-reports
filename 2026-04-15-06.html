Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad market strength led by semiconductor and leveraged tech plays—TQQQ +5.43%, SOXL +5.90%—with MU surging 9.2% on AI chip demand tailwinds and trillion-dollar valuation potential. Financial services showed mixed conviction: HOOD rocketed 10.4% on SEC's elimination of pattern day trader rules, COIN gained 5.7% riding Bitcoin past $75K, and ARES climbed 5.6% ahead of expected strong earnings; however, WFC dropped 5.7% after missing Q1 revenue targets. Energy reversed course as U.S.-Iran ceasefire hopes sparked rotation out of hedges, with APA off 6.2%. Industrials held steady with AXON (+5.9%), DAL (+6.9%), and TDG (+5.1%) benefiting from strong demand and earnings beats.

Recommendation

Buy** semiconductor exposure (MU, via SOXL) on sustained AI infrastructure demand—the 9%+ move suggests more upside. **Buy** HOOD on the structural tailwind from retail trading deregulation; this is a rare permanent rule change favoring the platform. **Avoid** energy names like APA until geopolitical clarity emerges, and stay cautious on beaten-down cloud infrastructure (AKAM) until Claude Managed Agents prove less disruptive than feared. **Hold** financial services broadly but trim WFC on relative underperformance.
