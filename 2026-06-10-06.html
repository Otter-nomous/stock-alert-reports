Market SummaryRead ▾

The semiconductor ecosystem dominated overnight gains, with SOXL surging 15.83% led by chipmakers and equipment suppliers. Intel (+11.2%), Micron (+9.9%), and KLA (+9.3%) outperformed on analyst upgrades, earnings beats, and AI infrastructure tailwinds, while equipment makers AMAT (+8.6%), LRCX (+7.0%), and ASML (+6.5%) benefited from raised guidance and price target hikes. Beyond chips, growth accelerated in crypto (COIN +6.4%, MSTR +5.6% on Bitcoin accumulation), healthcare (DXCM +5.2% on positive trial data), and selective industrials (FDXF +6.5%), suggesting broad risk-on sentiment despite SPY's modest +0.23% overnight move.

Recommendation

Ride the semiconductor momentum—KLAC, AMAT, and LRCX offer structural AI tailwinds with recent analyst validation, but INTC and MU are showing strong relative strength after recent underperformance. Avoid chasing fringe names like MSTR and COIN at these levels; the crypto rally appears speculative. Consider trimming Tech exposure if QQQ breaks above 2% gains, as the +1.56% overnight move suggests enthusiasm may be pricing in months of upside prematurely.
