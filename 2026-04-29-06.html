Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad-based semiconductor weakness dominated overnight action, with SOXL plummeting 11.21% as ARM (-8.0%), AMAT (-5.9%), MPWR (-5.3%), and TER (-5.4%) all sold off on US-China AI trade tensions and export control concerns. Tech weakness rippled across the board—GLW (-8.9%), LITE (-7.9%), and COHR (-5.5%) all retreated despite mixed earnings results, signaling investor anxiety over forward guidance. Healthcare split decisively: CNC surged 14% on BofA's double upgrade and strong guidance, while ZBH (-10.6%) and UHS (-9.5%) cratered despite earnings beats, suggesting the market punished weak forward outlooks. Industrials stumbled with PCAR (-6.0%) missing on revenue decline and PNR (-10.2%) cutting full-year guidance.

Recommendation

Buy the healthcare bifurcation:** CNC's 14% surge on guidance strength signals investors reward clarity and margin recovery—monitor similar names beating earnings with raised guidance. **Avoid semiconductor names until stabilization:** ARM, AMAT, and MPWR face structural headwinds from trade tensions and valuation resets that won't resolve overnight. **Sell into real estate weakness:** ARE's -11.3% drop on occupancy concerns reflects genuine sector stress; avoid exposure until life science REITs show stabilization. **Hold broad equity exposure:** SPY's modest -0.49% decline offers a reasonable entry point versus panic-driven sector selling.
