Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad equity weakness hit overnight with SPY -1.20%, QQQ -1.51%, and semiconductor stocks hammered—SOXL cratering 11.82% as ARM (-8.5%) faced FTC antitrust scrutiny and Intel (-6.2%) absorbed Jefferies' Mobileye downgrade. MU (-6.6%) and AMD (-5.7%) extended losses on China trade relief concerns and pre-Nvidia earnings anxiety respectively. Defensive bright spots emerged: DXCM (+6.6%) rallied on strong 2026 guidance and buyback, WDAY (+5.3%) and NOW (+5.0%) gained on AI integration wins, while FDS (+6.4%) attracted value hunters. Macro headwinds—rising Treasury yields and PPI inflation at 6%—pressured cyclicals like F (-7.5%), CHTR (-5.2%), and materials plays, though energy beneficiary APA (+5.0%) caught the oil price upside.

Recommendation

Avoid semiconductor exposure until regulatory clarity emerges on ARM and competitive positioning stabilizes post-Nvidia earnings Wednesday; SMCI, INTC, and AMD remain downside risks. Selectively buy AI software winners with pricing power (WDAY, NOW, DXCM) that are delivering margin expansion and growth—these are decoupling from hardware cycle weakness. Energy and undervalued financials (FDS) offer tactical opportunities if inflation fears moderate, but stay cautious on rate-sensitive telecom and automotive names.
