Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad market strength overnight, with QQQ +1.66% and TQQQ +4.91%, driven by a semiconductor supercycle fueled by Nvidia's bullish Arm commentary and easing geopolitical tensions. ARM soared 15.0% to all-time highs while the entire chip ecosystem rallied: AMD +8.1% (Taiwan $10B investment, 2nm EPYC ramp), INTC +7.4% (foundry traction), LRCX +6.8% (Morgan Stanley Overweight upgrade), ASML +6.2% (Goldman Sachs PT raise), and TER +7.1% (AI chip-testing demand doubling). Beyond semiconductors, energy and discretionary sectors benefited from falling oil prices and falling bond yields—UAL +10.0%, DAL +9.4%, NCLH +8.4%—while power utilities CEG +7.9%, NRG +8.3%, and VST +6.9% rallied on earnings beats and buyback completions.

Recommendation

Buy the semiconductor equipment leaders (LRCX, KLAC, ASML) on near-term strength**—analyst upgrades and rising capex forecasts justify momentum. **Avoid HAS (down 8.8% despite earnings beat) and consider taking profits on outsized movers like ARM and SMCI** if fundamentals don't support valuations at these levels. **Hold airline and cruise exposure (UAL, CCL, NCLH)** but watch oil prices closely; any reversal erases the fuel-cost tailwind that drove today's gains.
