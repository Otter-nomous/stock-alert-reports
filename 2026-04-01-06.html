Market SummaryRead ▾

Markets surged overnight as three major catalysts converged: Nvidia's $2 billion strategic investment in Marvell sparked a semiconductor sector explosion (MRVL +12.8%, ON +11.2%, SNDK +11.0%), ARM's projected $15 billion AGI chip revenue by 2031 lifted the licensing giant 10.5%, and Trump's reported consideration of ending Iran military conflict eased geopolitical risk premiums across cyclicals and risk assets. The Magnificent Seven and chip ecosystem benefited most—QQQ +3.39%, TQQQ +10.00%, SOXL +17.95%—while analyst endorsements from Bill Ackman (META +6.7%), Jim Cramer (JBL +7.3%, GLW +5.8%), and JPMorgan/Bernstein (STX +8.1%) amplified momentum in beaten-down names. Only two notable decliners emerged: CEG -6.5% (profit-taking after a 35.6% one-year run) and MKC -6.1% (Unilever merger skepticism).

Recommendation

BUY:** Semiconductor equipment and supply-chain plays (LRCX, AMAT, ASML, KLAC) remain structurally undervalued given Nvidia's $2B commitment and multi-year AI capex cycle; tactically, avoid chasing +10% overnight movers like MRVL and ON given overextension risk. **HOLD:** Large-cap tech (NVDA, META, GOOGL) on strength—position sizing matters more than entry now. **AVOID:** CEG on near-term profit-taking and defensive/staples (MKC) until geopolitical certainty fully embeds; watch for any Iran escalation reversal that could trigger sharp cyclical/energy sector corrections.
