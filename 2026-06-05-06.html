Broadcom reality check triggers tech selloff; financials and healthcare rally.
Market SummaryRead ▾

** Broadcom's (AVGO) 12.6% plunge despite beating earnings signals investor concern that AI chip demand expectations have become unsustainable, triggering a broad semiconductor rout that dragged down MU (-7.7%), LITE (-8.9%), and CIEN (-13.7%). The tech contagion spread to software and SaaS names—DDOG, NOW, and PANW all fell 5-7% despite solid fundamentals—as funds rotated out of richly-valued growth stocks. Meanwhile, healthcare stocks emerged as the day's clear winner, with MRNA (+7.5%) surging on cancer vaccine data and HUM, CNC, UNH, and MDT all climbing 5%+ on managed-care tailwinds and surgical innovation. Financials and REITs also found safe haven, with BX, KKR, and tower operators (AMT, SBAC, CCI) posting 5%+ gains.

Recommendation

** Avoid chasing fallen tech names—AVGO, MU, and CIEN are likely repricing AI demand downward and warrant caution. Healthcare offers genuine safety and catalysts: UNH (BofA upgrade + dividend hike) and MDT (Hugo robotics expansion) are worth nibbling. REITs look oversold on rate concerns and justify selective adds in data-center beneficiaries like AMT. Financial sector rotation appears healthy but not overdone; KKR's infrastructure play on AI power demand is tactically interesting for 3-6 month holding.
