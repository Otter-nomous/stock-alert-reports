Broad market futures are modestly higher this morning, with SPY up 0.17% and QQQ flat at +0.05%, though the semiconductor-heavy SOXL is notably down 3.84% despite individual chip wins. Micron (MU) surged 5.6% on D.A. Davidson's new Buy rating and $1,000 price target, driven by AI-fueled memory demand tailwinds, while Albemarle (ALB) gained 5.9% on renewed lithium optimism as EV demand stabilizes. Consumer stocks are stumbling—Domino's (DPZ) cratered 8.8% on Q1 earnings misses, and Dollar Tree (DLTR) fell 5.5% despite solid fundamentals, signaling sector-wide caution outweighing company-specific strength.

Recommendation

MU's AI-driven narrative and analyst upgrade offer near-term momentum; consider scaling in on dips rather than chasing at open. Avoid the consumer cyclical space for now—DPZ's earnings miss and DLTR's weakness despite strong results suggest margin compression and demand uncertainty are overriding individual company stories; wait for clearer sector stabilization before re-entering. ALB remains tactically interesting on EV recovery narratives but monitor commodity price strength before committing large positions.
