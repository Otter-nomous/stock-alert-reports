Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad-based tech strength lifted SPY +0.47%, QQQ +0.60%, and SOXL +3.91% overnight, with semiconductor and infrastructure plays leading the charge. APP surged 6.8% on mobile gaming ad tailwinds and Q1 revenue upgrades, while MPWR (+5.5%) benefited from AI demand thesis and VRSN (+5.6%) gained on earnings optimism. Storage and infrastructure plays rallied—STX +5.6% following Morgan Stanley's "Top Pick" upgrade citing HDD market recovery—but sentiment splits emerged with LITE down 6.6% on valuation concerns after its 1,400% year-long run and SMCI down 5.0% amid legal headwinds over alleged China-related server diversions.

Recommendation

Buy into semiconductor and AI infrastructure strength (MPWR, VRSN, STX) where analyst upgrades reflect genuine demand cycles, but avoid catching falling knives like LITE given extreme valuation extension and SMCI until legal uncertainty clears. MSTR's +6.6% crypto-fueled bounce offers tactical opportunity for risk-on traders, but avoid bottom-fishing SMCI until the company addresses securities litigation—downside risk outweighs reward at current levels.
