Market SummaryRead ▾

The market is embracing a tech-led rally fueled by AI infrastructure momentum, with semiconductor and software stocks dominating overnight gains. ORCL (+12.0%) and SNDK (+11.0%) led the charge on AI tools and index inclusion tailwinds, while design software names CDNS (+7.8%), SNPS (+6.1%), and ARM (+5.1%) rallied on expanded AI partnerships with Nvidia. Enterprise software benefited from "buy the dip" calls—NOW, WDAY, and CRWD all gained 6%+—and fintech benefited from PayPal's Canva partnership (+5.6%) and FIS's prediction market clearing product (+6.4%). The lone casualty was FAST (-6.8%), which fell despite beating sales despite meeting earnings, signaling that margin concerns override headline beats.

Recommendation

Buy the semiconductor-AI nexus (CDNS, SNPS, ARM) and oversold enterprise software on weakness (NOW, WDAY), but trim ORCL and SNDK after 12% and 11% overnight surges—momentum-driven index flows may reverse. Avoid FAST and watch financial services for rotation exhaustion; while KKR, BX, and ARES rallied, redemption pressures and valuation resets remain structural headwinds. Position into quality defensive names (FICO +8.2%) only if geopolitical concerns persist.
