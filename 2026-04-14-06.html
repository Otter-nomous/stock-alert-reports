Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad-based tech rally drove overnight gains across all major indices, with QQQ +1.03%, SPY +0.98%, and semiconductor-heavy SOXL +5.46%. Oracle (+12.7%) led on expanded Bloom Energy fuel cell partnership for AI data centers, while SanDisk (+11.8%) spiked on NASDAQ-100 inclusion. The SaaS sector rebounded sharply—Cadence and FICO each +8.5%, ServiceNow +7.3%, Atlassian +7.3%—fueled by oversold valuations, U.S.-Iran ceasefire optimism, and renewed AI infrastructure demand. Financial services followed suit with KKR +7.6%, Blackstone +6.1%, and Ares Management +6.2%. The sole notable loser was Fastenal (FAST, -6.9%) on Q1 2026 earnings disappointment despite sales growth.

Recommendation

Buy the AI infrastructure complex (ORCL, SNPS, ARM, CDNS) on sustained data center capex tailwinds, and selectively add oversold SaaS names (TEAM, NOW, WDAY, INTU) that have solid AI positioning. Avoid chasing speculative M&A rumors (DELL, HPQ on alleged Nvidia interest—already denied). Monitor Fastenal and similar cyclical industrials for margin recovery; today's weakness may offer entry points if pricing stabilizes.
