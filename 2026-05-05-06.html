Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad market weakness overnight (-0.19% to -0.41% across major indices) masked a sharp sectoral rotation away from logistics. Amazon's new Supply Chain Services platform triggered a logistics bloodbath: UPS (-10.5%), FedEx (-9.1%), and C.H. Robinson (-9.1%) all sold off sharply, while freight forwarder EXPD (-5.1%) declined on caution ahead of Q1 earnings. Conversely, semiconductor strength remained the bright spot with Micron (+6.3%) and Sandisk (+5.8%) surging on AI-driven memory demand and supply tightness. Elsewhere, TSN rallied 8.0% on strong earnings guidance, INSM jumped 5.1% on bullish analyst upgrades, and COIN gained 6.1% on stablecoin regulatory clarity, while AMD disappointed post-rally (-5.3%) and cruise lines took a hit from fuel costs (NCLH -8.6%).

Recommendation

Avoid the entire logistics/freight sector until Amazon's competitive threat is better understood—UPS, FDX, and CHRW face structural headwinds and margin pressure. Selectively accumulate semiconductor exposure (MU, SNDK) on any dips given durable AI tailwinds and pricing power, but be cautious on AMD given elevated expectations. Consumer Defensive (TSN) and specialty healthcare (INSM) offer relative safety; COIN offers a cleaner entry on regulatory clarity if crypto volatility allows.
