Broad equity weakness is gripping the market overnight, with SPY down 0.54%, QQQ off 0.43%, and the leveraged TQQQ sliding 1.28%—signaling modest tech headwinds. The notable outlier is SOXL, rallying 1.84% and pointing to semiconductor strength despite overall market softness. In single-stock action, BLDR (Builders FirstSource) tanked 5.5% on weak Q1 earnings and net losses amid housing slowdown concerns, while EL (Estée Lauder) dropped 5.2% following an analyst downgrade. The divergence suggests defensive positioning in discretionary consumer stocks while semiconductor demand remains resilient.

Recommendation

Avoid BLDR and EL on today's weakness—both face structural headwinds (housing and luxury demand) that downgrades are only beginning to price in. Conversely, semiconductor strength warrants cautious accumulation in quality semi names, as SOXL's outperformance indicates institutional conviction in the sector despite broader tech malaise. Hold SPY/QQQ positions but use this dip to rotate into beaten-down chip exposure rather than chase the tech selloff.
