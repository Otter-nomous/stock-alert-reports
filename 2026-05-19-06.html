Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad semiconductor and memory chip selling pressured QQQ (-0.43%) and TQQQ (-1.35%), with SOXL cratering -7.57% as MU (-6.0%), AMAT (-5.3%), and SNDK (-5.3%) retreated on valuation concerns and factory capacity warnings from Seagate (-6.9%). In contrast, enterprise AI and software plays rallied sharply: CTSH (+9.1%), NOW (+8.8%), EPAM (+8.0%), and ACN (+5.2%) all gained on AI services demand and analyst upgrades, while healthcare beneficiaries posted mixed results (REGN -9.8% on trial failure, but DXCM +5.6% and BSX +6.2% on strong guidance). The standout: Dominion Energy (+9.4%) surged on NextEra's $66.8B all-stock acquisition, creating the world's largest utility positioned for AI-driven power demand.

Recommendation

Rotate away from commodity-like semiconductor exposure (avoid AMAT, MU, SNDK near-term) and lean into capital-light AI software and services—CTSH, NOW, EPAM, and ACN all show stronger secular tailwinds and less crowded valuations than chip plays. Consider D if the NextEra deal closes smoothly, as utilities are positioned for multi-year AI infrastructure buildout; avoid speculative semiconductor equipment plays until clarity emerges on factory timelines and memory demand sustainability.
