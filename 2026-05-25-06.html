Market SummaryRead ▾

Equities are poised for a solid open as semiconductor and AI-adjacent names dominate overnight gains, with SOXL jumping 6.82% on strength across DELL (+16.8%), HPQ (+15.3%), NTAP (+12.4%), and SWKS (+12.1%). The semiconductor surge reflects dual catalysts: easing US-China trade tensions following Trump's Beijing tech summit signaling potential chip export relief, plus surging enterprise demand for AI infrastructure and data center hardware from hyperscalers. Beyond chips, ROST's 8.1% pop on a 17.6% EPS beat signals consumer resilience, while falling Treasury yields are rekindling appetite for growth stocks like WDAY (+5.2%) and TTD (+5.2%), and lifting rate-sensitive auto plays like F (+9.2%).

Recommendation

Buy:** Accumulate positions in semiconductor leaders (QCOM, NXPI, ON) and AI infrastructure beneficiaries (DELL, HPE, NTAP) on dips—the chip export relief narrative and hyperscaler capex cycle remain intact. **Hold/Trim:** ROST and consumer cyclicals have had a strong run; lock in gains on the 8%+ move and reassess on weakness. **Avoid:** Super Micro (SMCI) despite the 6.3% pop—compliance scrutiny remains a drag; better opportunities exist in cleaner names like DELL and HPE with stronger governance profiles.
