Market SummaryRead ▾

The semiconductor sector is in freefall after Broadcom's disappointing forward guidance spooked institutional investors, with chip heavyweights ARM (-12.3%), MRVL (-11.8%), MU (-11.3%), and INTC (-10.1%) leading a brutal selloff. A stronger-than-expected jobs report has reignited Fed rate-hike expectations, sending Treasury yields higher and pressuring the entire QQQ (-4.32%) and TQQQ (-12.97%) complex. Broader rotation is hitting richly-valued mega-cap tech—NVDA (-5.6%), META (-5.7%), TSLA (-6.5%)—while semiconductor equipment makers like LRCX (-8.5%), AMAT (-8.0%), and ASML (-5.5%) are caught in the crossfire despite underlying AI demand fundamentals. Profit-taking is evident across winners that rallied hard YTD (ON -9.0%, GNRC -6.9%, NEM -7.0%), suggesting investors are locking in gains amid valuation anxiety.

Recommendation

Avoid:** Semiconductor and equipment stocks at current levels—wait for stabilization before re-entering; the sector is pricing in demand destruction, not just correction. **Hold/Trim:** Large-cap tech (NVDA, META, TSLA) likely see near-term pressure until rate expectations cool or earnings justify valuations. **Selective Buy:** Counter-trend winners like COO (+8.2%) and defensively-positioned names with positive catalysts unrelated to AI/chip cycles merit small positions, but avoid chasing into this weakness—cash is the best position until volatility subsides.
