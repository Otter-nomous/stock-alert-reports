Broad equity indices are holding steady overnight (SPY -0.02%, QQQ -0.11%), but semiconductor weakness is dragging: SOXL down 3.46% with QCOM (-6.2%), ARM (-5.8%), and SWKS (-5.7%) all retreating as investors rotate toward pure-play AI chip leaders. Healthcare took a beating with BSX plunging 12.5% on disappointing guidance and PODD falling 5.1% due to a major pump recall. Offsetting losses, consumer cyclicals and select tech names rallied: APP surged 10.4% on analyst upgrades, MGM jumped 9.1% following a JPMorgan upgrade, and CDW gained 5.2% on an earnings beat and buyback. Energy stocks declined 5%+ (BKR) as crude tumbled on Iran-US peace deal progress.

Recommendation

Sell healthcare exposure** — BSX's guidance miss signals broader sector weakness, and PODD's recall suggests regulatory headwinds ahead. **Avoid semiconductor diversification plays** (QCOM, SWKS) in favor of proven AI infrastructure names. **Buy on dips**: APP and CDW show analyst conviction and solid fundamentals; MGM's JPMorgan upgrade and improving Vegas trends offer near-term upside. Consider trimming energy holdings until geopolitical clarity improves.
