Market SummaryRead ▾

Tech led overnight gains as Middle East de-escalation and eased geopolitical tensions triggered a sharp rotation into high-growth equities. QQQ (+1.40%) and TQQQ (+4.19%) outpaced broader indices, with growth names like TEAM (+10.5%), DASH (+10.0%), and DDOG (+9.5%) surging on renewed investor appetite for AI and margin-expansion narratives. Separately, regulatory relief—the SEC's removal of day-trader minimum balance requirements—lifted HOOD (+10.4%), while specific stock catalysts such as Tesla's Terafab chip initiative (+7.6%), Barclays' DoorDash autonomous-delivery thesis (+10.0%), and Jim Cramer's ServiceNow endorsement (+7.3%) added fuel to the rally. Defensives and cyclicals tied to geopolitical risk took pressure: CARR (-9.4%), LII (-6.9%), IR (-5.6%), and AOS (-5.3%) all declined as investors rotated away from conflict-hedged positions.

Recommendation

Lock in gains on oversold SaaS and high-beta tech names (TEAM, DDOG, WDAY) that rallied sharply overnight—momentum often reverses on relief rallies. Avoid catching falling knives in defensive industrials (CARR, LII, IR) until stabilization signals emerge. HOOD's regulatory catalyst is structurally positive for retail trading volume; consider nibbling if today's momentum holds, but watch for profit-taking at open.
