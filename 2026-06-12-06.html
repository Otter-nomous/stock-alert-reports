Market SummaryRead ▾

The overnight session saw explosive semiconductor strength led by SNDK (+14.5%), KLAC (+12.9%), LRCX (+12.7%), and MU (+11.7%), as AI memory demand and infrastructure spending catalysts collided with Trump's reported Iran peace deal announcement. This geopolitical relief eased rate pressure across tech, lifting QQQ +3.38% and driving massive outperformance in semiconductor equipment (SOXL +23.99%). Beyond chips, the rally broadened into industrials (airlines and defense up 6-9%), consumer cyclicals (cruises, housing), and materials (copper, lithium), signaling risk-on sentiment. Notable downgrades hit software: ADBE (-6.2%) and ADSK (-7.1%) fell on AI monetization concerns, while ORCL (-8.5%) slumped despite solid earnings due to massive capex spending guidance.

Recommendation

Buy:** Semiconductor equipment makers (KLAC, LRCX, AMAT) remain the cleanest AI infrastructure play with multi-year visibility; MU's doubled price target signals institutional conviction on memory demand. **Avoid:** Software names facing AI disruption (ADBE, ADSK, WDAY) until monetization clarity emerges—these declines foreshadow margin pressure. **Hold/Monitor:** Airlines and discretionary cyclicals jumped on geopolitical relief, but sentiment can reverse quickly; lock in gains on UAL, DAL, CCL if you're holding.
