Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad tech weakness (-0.68% QQQ) was driven by Anthropic's Claude autonomous AI announcement, which triggered sharp selloffs in enterprise software: Atlassian (-8.4%), Workday (-5.7%), ServiceNow (-5.7%), Salesforce (-6.2%), and Intuit (-5.4%) all retreated on automation displacement concerns. However, semiconductor and infrastructure plays surged—Lumentum (+10.0%) and Corning (+8.4%) rallied on AI data center tailwinds and analyst upgrades, while Trump's $4 trillion semiconductor supply chain plan lifted Teradyne (+5.3%) and Seagate (+5.2%). Meanwhile, chemicals and materials benefited from geopolitical risk premium: LyondellBasell (+6.4%), Dow (+6.3%), and Albemarle (+5.7%) climbed on Middle East tensions boosting petrochemical and lithium demand.

Recommendation

Avoid the beaten-down enterprise software cohort (TEAM, WDAY, CRM, NOW) until AI displacement fears clarify—these stocks are pricing in worst-case scenarios. Instead, rotate into semiconductor infrastructure (LUMENTUM, LITE, GLW, CIEN) and chemicals (LYB, DOW) where fundamental tailwinds (data center demand, supply constraints) offer clearer near-term catalysts. Hold hardware beneficiaries like DELL (+7.5%) and cyclical materials on continued geopolitical risk and supply chain reshoring momentum.
