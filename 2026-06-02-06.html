Market SummaryRead ▾

Tech stocks dominated overnight gains as Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's reassurance on AI disruption sparked a broad software rally, lifting WDAY (+7.6%), CRM (+9.7%), and NOW (+9.2%), while semiconductor plays capitalized on infrastructure demand—ARM surged 15.7% on PC chip licensing upside, MU gained 6.6% on Raymond James' doubled price target, and DELL jumped 10.7% on Morgan Stanley's 164% target hike. However, QCOM fell 8.8% as Nvidia's competing PC chips threaten its processor dominance, and META declined 5.1% on Alphabet's $80 billion stock issuance signaling escalating AI capex concerns. Outside tech, MGM soared 16.1% on Barry Diller's $18 billion takeover bid, while cybersecurity names (CRWD +7.0%, PANW +6.7%, FTNT +6.6%) and data infrastructure plays (DDOG +12.2%, CDW +12.4%) rallied on sector momentum.

Recommendation

Buy:** ARM, DDOG, and semiconductor/AI infrastructure beneficiaries (MU, ANET, LITE) remain attractive on structural chip demand; consider DELL for enterprise AI capex exposure. **Avoid:** QCOM faces genuine competitive pressure from Nvidia's PC chips and warrants caution; META's 5.1% decline on capex inflation concerns suggests waiting for clarity on AI ROI. **Hold:** Broad software rally is healthy, but valuations now reflect optimism—take profits on +9-10% gainers like CRM and NOW rather than chase further upside.
