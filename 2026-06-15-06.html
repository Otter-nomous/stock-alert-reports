Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks dominated overnight action as the broader tech sector rallied on easing U.S.-Iran tensions and sustained AI infrastructure demand. ARM led the charge with an 11.3% surge following a Bank of America upgrade, while memory chip plays STX, WDC, and SNDK gained 7.3%, 6.4%, and 5.2% respectively on structural supply constraints. Semiconductor equipment makers KLAC (+5.5%) and TER (+5.7%) also benefited from reduced geopolitical risk premium. The only notable tech casualty was ADBE (-6.8%), hit by multiple analyst downgrades, while SATS (-11.0%) cratered on SpaceX IPO disappointment. Basic materials rallied too—MOS (+7.6%) and ALB (+7.1%) gained on commodity tailwinds—but the session was unmistakably semiconductor-driven.

Recommendation

The chip sector's momentum looks durable given genuine AI capex cycles and geopolitical de-risking; consider adding to semiconductor equipment and memory positions if volatility allows. Avoid ADBE until the stock stabilizes and shows catalysts to justify current valuations. SATS presents a speculative recovery opportunity if SpaceX valuation stabilizes, but CVNA (-5.5%) should remain on the sidelines pending clarity on its securitization health.
