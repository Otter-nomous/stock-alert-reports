Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad tech weakness is dragging major indices lower overnight, with QQQ down 0.85% and semiconductor names particularly hammered. Memory and storage stocks (SNDK -6.2%, WDC -5.3%) are retreating as hotter-than-expected inflation data (3.8% annually) spooks investors and halts the memory supercycle rally. Qualcomm (-11.5%) and other legacy chip players are underperforming as capital rotates into Nvidia following Jensen Huang's high-profile placement in Trump's China trade delegation. Offsetting some losses: strong earnings from ZBRA (+11.4%) and Q (+9.9%) show selective strength in well-positioned semiconductor materials plays, while INSM (+11.7%) rebounds as healthcare investors view last week's post-earnings decline as overdone.

Recommendation

Avoid broad semiconductor exposure until inflation concerns clarify—WDC, SNDK, and QCOM look vulnerable to further rotation. Consider selective entry into earnings winners like ZBRA and Q on dips, which are proving resilient despite sector headwinds. Hold tech index shorts until the inflation narrative settles; otherwise, wait for capitulation before re-engaging growth equities.
