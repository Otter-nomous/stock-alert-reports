Markets are opening sharply lower across the board, with SPY down 1.70%, QQQ down 1.85%, and leveraged tech plays like TQQQ and SOXL bleeding 5-6%. Recommendation

Avoid tech and semiconductor exposure until SMCI headlines stabilize and supply chain concerns clarify—the sector is in a vicious feedback loop. Consider rotating into oversold defensive names like IRM (down 5.1% on profit-taking, not fundamentals) and monitor healthcare for capitulation buys if weakness persists. Hold cash for better entry points; today's breadth deterioration suggests more downside before stabilization.
