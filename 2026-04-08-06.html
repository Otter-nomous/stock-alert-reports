Market SummaryRead ▾

Technology stocks led overnight gains, with semiconductor and networking names driving broad strength: AVGO (+6.2%), ANET (+5.9%), and LITE (+5.6%) benefited from AI infrastructure demand and geopolitical risk reduction following the Trump-Iran ceasefire. Healthcare insurers posted exceptional gains on CMS's finalized Medicare Advantage rate increases for 2027—UNH (+9.4%), HUM (+7.9%), and CVS (+6.7%) all surged on improved profitability visibility. PSKY (+10.7%) led Communication Services after securing $24B in Middle Eastern funding for its Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition, while TTD (-6.8%) stumbled on executive departures. Broad indices remain modestly higher (SPX +0.08%, SPY +0.04%), with semiconductor leverage SOXL outperforming at +3.17%.

Recommendation

Healthcare insurers offer compelling near-term value following the Medicare catalyst—accumulate UNH and HUM on any pullback, as rate increases provide multi-year earnings tailwinds. Technology infrastructure plays (ANET, LITE, AVGO) remain attractive given sustained AI capex cycles, though valuations warrant disciplined entry. Avoid TTD until leadership transitions clarify; monitor M&A names like PSKY for execution risk despite strong financing momentum.
