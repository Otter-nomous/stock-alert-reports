Market SummaryRead ▾

A 4.2% CPI reading—the highest in three years—reignited Fed rate hike concerns and sparked a broad selloff across growth equities overnight. The semiconductor sector bore the brunt, with SOXL cratering 10.43%, driven by losses in QCOM (-6.9%), ARM (-5.4%), MRVL (-5.4%), and ON (-5.8%). Beyond chips, industrials (GNRC -8.4%, CAT -6.4%, CMI -5.8%) and consumer cyclicals (IP -6.7%, CCL -6.3%) rolled over on valuation concerns. The outlier: SMCI's catastrophic -28% plunge and selective semiconductor liquidation appear driven by retail cash raising for the anticipated SpaceX IPO, not fundamentals alone.

Recommendation

Avoid semiconductor and high-multiple growth stocks until inflation trajectory clarifies and Fed signaling stabilizes—this is a duration correction. DVN's +5.7% rally on shareholder returns and energy's relative resilience suggest tactical rotation into value and cyclicals. Buy the dip selectively in quality semis (AVGO, MRVL) only after stabilization signals emerge; until then, hold cash or rotate into DVN-like energy plays offering near-term cash yield and downside protection.
