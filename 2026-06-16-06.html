Market SummaryRead ▾

Overnight markets surged on Trump's announced Iran peace deal reopening the Strait of Hormuz, driving broad risk-on sentiment across tech and consumer cyclicals. Semiconductors led gains—ARM +11.3% on BofA's 37% price target upgrade, INTC +6.5% on AI data center strength, STX +7.3% on Morgan Stanley's bullish call—while delivery and travel plays rallied on lower oil and yields (DASH +11.6%, ABNB +5.0%, RCL +6.6%). However, communication services cratered as Fox's $22B Roku acquisition (FOXA -16.8%, FOX -15.2%) triggered shareholder panic over the strategic bet, while ADBE -6.8% reflects persistent AI competition fears in enterprise software. QQQ's +3.14% overnight surge and SOXL's +4.77% pop confirm tech leadership.

Recommendation

Buy the dip in semiconductor strength**—ARM, INTC, and WDC remain well-positioned on AI tailwinds and geopolitical relief; their overnight gains have room to run if yields stay compressed. **Avoid Fox's acquisition story** entirely; the 15%+ selloff signals genuine shareholder skepticism that won't reverse quickly. **Hold or trim ADBE** unless valuations reset further—enterprise software faces real headwinds that one peace deal won't cure.
