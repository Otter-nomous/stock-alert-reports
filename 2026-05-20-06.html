Market SummaryRead ▾

Overnight weakness gripped the broad market with SPY and QQQ both down roughly 0.65%, signaling renewed pressure from rising bond yields on growth stocks. Technology absorbed the heaviest blows—AKAM dropped 6.3% amid sector headwinds, while FSLR tumbled 5.1% despite favorable renewable energy tailwinds, outpaced by stronger competitors SolarEdge (+22%) and Enphase (+11%). Notably, semiconductor infrastructure plays like SOXL bucked the trend with a modest +0.09% gain, suggesting selective rotation into economically resilient segments rather than broad tech capitulation.

Recommendation

Avoid AKAM and FSLR at current levels; today's declines reflect crowding and relative underperformance rather than isolated weakness. Conversely, semiconductor and data center infrastructure stocks (evidenced by SOXL's resilience) warrant nibbling on dips given their defensive positioning amid yield uncertainty. Hold broad market exposure through SPY/QQQ but monitor bond yields closely—a sustained move above 4.5% on the 10-year could trigger deeper tech selloffs.
