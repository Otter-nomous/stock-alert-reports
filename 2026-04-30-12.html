Market SummaryRead ▾

The market is pricing in a structural shift toward AI-driven capital spending, with broad strength across semiconductors, networking, and data center infrastructure stocks. QCOM (+15.1%), PWR (+15.5%), CAT (+10.3%), and CARR (+8.1%) all surged on explosive data center order growth (500%+ in some cases), while optical and cooling plays like CIEN (+10.2%), GLW (+7.8%), and VRT (+7.5%) capitalized on hyperscaler demand acceleration. Alphabet's +9% pop on strong Cloud growth and Eli Lilly's +10.4% jump on GLP-1 momentum added breadth. The sole cautionary note: META's -7.8% drop despite earnings beats, as investors balk at the company's $125–145B full-year capex guidance, signaling potential margin compression ahead.

Recommendation

Buy industrials and semiconductor names riding the AI infrastructure wave (PWR, CAT, CARR, CIEN, GLW) as backlog visibility remains exceptional; these have more upside if capex cycles extend. Avoid or trim META here—the stock's valuation no longer prices in the magnitude of near-term margin pressure from elevated AI spending. Hold QQQ/SPY for broad exposure, but rotate overweight toward capex beneficiaries and away from mega-cap narrative stocks until the capex-to-profitability equation clarifies.
