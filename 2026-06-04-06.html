Market SummaryRead ▾

Broad tech selloff overnight (-0.26% QQQ, -0.70% SPY) driven by US-Iran escalation concerns and renewed inflation fears from oil approaching $98/bbl, triggering sector-wide profit-taking despite positive earnings. Software stocks bore the brunt with NOW (-7.6%), DDOG (-7.0%), MSTR (-7.0%), and PLTR (-6.6%) all declining despite company-specific catalysts, while enterprise discretionary (CHTR -8.0%, CMCSA -5.4%) and payments (GPN -8.3%, COIN -6.2%) also weakened. Contrarian strength emerged in AI infrastructure plays (SNDK +6.7%, WDC +5.5% on Citigroup's 37% upgrade) and healthcare (MRNA +7.5% on UBS upgrade, MDT +5.7% post-earnings), plus energy/industrials (TPL +9.7%, URI +6.2%) benefiting from commodity tailwinds.

Recommendation

Avoid chasing tech names lower—software sector weakness is broad and macro-driven, making further downside likely; skip PANW, ORCL, and CRM today. Rotate into undervalued AI infrastructure beneficiaries (WDC, SNDK) and industrial/healthcare names showing strength on fundamentals (MDT, URI, MRNA) as defensive positioning ahead of geopolitical clarity. Hold quality SaaS with durable growth if already owned, but do not add until volatility settles and inflation narrative shifts.
