Market SummaryRead ▾

The semiconductor sector is on fire, with SOXL surging 16.33% overnight as memory and chip stocks lead the charge. Micron (+15.5%) and SanDisk (+16.6%) are riding AI-driven demand and Samsung's supply constraints, while Intel (+14.0%) and AMD (+11.4%) benefit from broad chipmaker optimism and potential Apple partnerships. Beyond chips, Akamai's massive $1.8B Anthropic deal (+26.6%), Monster Beverage's earnings beat (+13.6%), and Dell's Trump endorsement (+13.1%) powered individual movers, but weakness in MercadoLibre (-12.7%), Mettler-Toledo (-14.8%), and Motorola (-11.4%) signals selective profit-taking in pockets of the market.

Recommendation

Buy weakness in quality semiconductor names (INTC, AMD, QCOM) if today's gains pull back—the AI infrastructure thesis remains intact with earnings beats validating demand. Avoid chasing already-extended energy drink and consumer discretionary plays after Monster and Dell's outsized rallies, and stay cautious on names showing guidance misses (MSI, FIS) despite earnings beats, as forward momentum is what matters in this market.
