Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks led a broad-based technology advance, with DELL (+16.8%), HPQ (+15.3%), NTAP (+12.4%), SWKS (+12.1%), and QCOM (+11.6%) all posting double-digit gains on AI infrastructure demand and positive earnings momentum. The rally extends across hardware and software—NXP and ON beat earnings with strong guidance, while growth stocks like WDAY and TTD recovered as Treasury yields cooled, triggering a risk-on rotation. Outside tech, tailwinds reached consumer cyclicals (Ford +9.2% on retail momentum; Carvana +6.0% on analyst upgrade) and industrials (Generac +9.0% on data center tailwinds), while EL's +11.9% surge followed deal relief.

Recommendation

Semiconductor and AI infrastructure plays remain the primary opportunity—DELL, NTAP, and ON offer near-term momentum with fundamental support, making them hold/accumulate positions. Avoid chasing retail-driven momentum in F and speculative names like CVNA; instead, rotate into quality hardware beneficiaries with analyst backing. Watch whether today's yield-driven rally sustains; if yields drop further, double down on growth; if they revert, reduce exposure to unprofitable SaaS names trading on sentiment alone.
