Market SummaryRead ▾

Semiconductor stocks are commanding the session with SOXL surging 13.02%, driven by broad-based strength across memory and chip equipment names: INTC +12.9%, SNDK +12.0%, MU +11.1%, QCOM +10.8%, LRCX +6.7%, and TER +5.8%. The catalyst is AMD's strong Q1 beat and bullish AI infrastructure guidance, expanding conviction beyond GPUs into storage, memory, and equipment supply chains. However, SHOP's 15.6% collapse signals that the market is ruthless on growth deceleration—despite 34% revenue growth, slowing earnings disappointed investors. Alongside SHOP's stumble, PYPL (-7.7%), PLTR (-6.9%), and FISV (-8.8%) fell on profitability or guidance concerns, while winners like WAT (+13.5%), ROK (+8.9%), and IQV (+9.6%) beat across the board with credible forward guidance.

Recommendation

Buy into semiconductor weakness tactically—INTC, MU, and LRCX remain structurally sound on AI demand, and today's rally has room to run if equipment utilization sustains. Avoid SHOP, PYPL, and PLTR until management demonstrates earnings acceleration, not just cost-cutting; the market is pricing zero tolerance for margin-focused stories. Rotate into quality earnings beats with raised guidance (ROK, IQV, WAT) as these offer safety in a bifurcated market.
