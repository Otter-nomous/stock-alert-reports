Market SummaryRead ▾

Overnight weakness is concentrated in semiconductors and optical communications, with SOXL cratering 10.51% following OpenAI's disappointing user/revenue growth and U.S.-China AI technology tensions. ARM (-7.6%), AMAT (-5.0%), MPWR (-5.3%), and SNDK (-5.1%) all collapsed on dampened chip demand outlook and geopolitical headwinds, while Corning's weak forward guidance triggered a cascade sell-off in optical peers LITE (-8.3%), CIEN (-7.2%), and COHR (-5.9%). Beyond tech, earnings beats are being punished across industrials and healthcare—PACCAR, Pentair, ZBH, and UHS all fell despite beating estimates, signaling investor skepticism on guidance and forward demand. Bright spots remain rare: CNC +13.1% on raised 2026 guidance, BEN +6.4% on strong net inflows, and NUE +5.1% on tariff-driven steel tailwinds.

Recommendation

Avoid semiconductor and optical communications names until sentiment stabilizes—the thesis has shifted from AI tailwind to demand destruction and geopolitical risk. Consider rotating into defensive winners like CNC (proven execution with raised guidance) or NUE (structural tariff support), but hold cash on broad-market weakness given SPY's -0.50% and QQQ's -0.93% overnight declines. Watch for capitulation signals in SOXL and AMAT before re-entering tech exposure, as current selling appears driven by guidance disappointment rather than fundamental deterioration.
